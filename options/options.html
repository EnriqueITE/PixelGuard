VirusTotal

Optional manual lookups using your own API key. Opening messages or saving settings never sends a query.

When you request a lookup, PixelGuard sends your API key and the selected domain, full URL or attachment SHA-256 directly to VirusTotal over HTTPS. No email body or attachment content is uploaded. Full URLs may contain private identifiers; even hashes can identify known files.

The key is stored locally in your Thunderbird profile, without synchronization or an encryption guarantee. It is excluded from PixelGuard exports and logs.

Public keys are limited to 4 requests per minute and 500 per day. Commercial products and certain business workflows are restricted; your use must comply with your VirusTotal license.
